Our Story

Engineering the Future

Learn about who we are, where we come from, and what drives us forward in the world of robotics.

Youth Village Meir Shfeya

PlanTech #17106 was founded in 2019 at the Meir Shfeya Youth Village in Israel. We are a dedicated group of students in grades 9-12 who share a passion for STEM, engineering, and problem-solving.

Over the years, we've grown not just as engineers, but as a family. We design, build, and program our robots together, overcoming challenges and celebrating our victories. We are proud to represent our school and our country, especially as we head to the Michiana Premier Event in the USA!
